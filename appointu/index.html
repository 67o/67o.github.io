Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Appointu free to use for customers?
Yes! Appointu is 100% free for customers to discover nearby businesses, check slot timings, and book digital appointment tokens without any convenience fee.
How do payments work? Do I pay in the app?
Appointu is a strictly non-transactional platform. There are no in-app payments or online payment gateways. You pay your bill directly at the physical shop or clinic after your service is completed.
How does live token tracking work?
When you book an appointment, you receive a unique digital token number. As the shopkeeper marks earlier customers as "Served" in their dashboard, your app screen automatically updates in real-time so you know exactly when to arrive.
How can I register my shop or clinic as a Service Provider?
Simply download the Appointu app, select the "Service Provider" role during onboarding, enter your business details, upload your shop photos and verification proof (Shop Act/ID), and our team will verify your listing within 24 hours.
Is my personal data safe?
Absolutely. We adhere to the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act, India). All data is secured on Google Firebase and Cloudinary with AES-256 encryption. We never sell your data to third parties.
