Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about Niyamam.

Is Niyamam free to use?
Yes! Niyamam is completely free with all core habit tracking, voice reminders, and streak features available out of the box.
How do voice reminders work?
When a routine alarm triggers, Niyamam uses the on-device Text-to-Speech engine to announce the task title and an inspiring motivational quote.
Does it need an internet connection?
No. Niyamam operates 100% offline. All your data is safely saved in local SQLite storage on your phone.
